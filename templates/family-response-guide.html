Family Response Guide
Criminal Detention in China

When a loved one is detained, every hour matters. This guide outlines the critical steps family members must take within the first 48 hours to protect their rights and secure the best possible outcome.

URGENT: If your family member has been detained within the last 48 hours, contact our emergency criminal defense line immediately: +86 186 0163 6266 or email m.tan@sofoslaw.cn

1

Confirm the Detention Details

Upon receiving a detention notice (拘留通知书), verify the following immediately:

  • Detention location: The notice will specify which detention center (看守所) is holding the suspect
  • Alleged offense: The statutory charge (涉嫌罪名) and applicable Criminal Law article
  • Detaining authority: Confirm whether it is Public Security Bureau (公安局), Procuratorate (检察院), or other authority
  • Date of detention: This determines when the 37-day maximum detention period begins

Important: Under Chinese law, the police must notify family within 24 hours of detention unless notification would hinder the investigation. If you have not received a notice within 48 hours, demand one from the detaining authority.

2

Engage a Criminal Defense Lawyer Immediately

Only a licensed defense lawyer may meet with a detained suspect during the investigation phase (侦查阶段). This is critical:

  • Lawyers can meet with suspects within 48 hours of engagement
  • Family members cannot meet with the suspect until the case concludes
  • Early lawyer engagement allows for immediate rights protection and evidence preservation
  • A lawyer can apply for bail (取保候审) during the investigation phase

When selecting a lawyer, prioritize those with demonstrated experience in the specific charge category (e.g., embezzlement, fraud, cybercrime, DUI).

3

Preserve Evidence of Mitigating Circumstances

Collect and organize all documents that may support a reduced sentence or probation:

  • Employment records: Contracts, performance reviews, tenure documentation
  • Financial records: Bank statements showing restitution capability
  • Character references: Letters from employers, community leaders, family
  • Medical records: If applicable, documentation of health conditions
  • Awards and recognitions: Professional achievements and community service
4

Understand the Investigation Phase Timeline

Chinese criminal procedure follows strict statutory timelines. Understanding these helps set expectations:

  • Initial detention: Up to 30 days (extendable to 37 days with Procuratorate approval)
  • Arrest decision: The Procuratorate must decide whether to approve arrest within 7 days of receiving the request
  • Post-arrest investigation: Typically 2 months (extendable)
  • Prosecution review: 1 month (extendable by 15 days)
  • Trial: First-instance courts must decide within 2-3 months

Critical window: The period between detention and formal arrest (up to 37 days) is the most important phase for intervention. Securing bail or a non-arrest decision during this window dramatically improves outcomes.

5

Consider Restitution and Victim Forgiveness

For economic crimes (embezzlement, fraud), restitution and victim forgiveness are among the most powerful mitigation tools:

  • Full restitution of misappropriated funds significantly improves sentencing outcomes
  • Victim forgiveness (谅解书) can reduce sentences by 20-40%
  • Your lawyer can negotiate with victims or their representatives to secure forgiveness agreements
  • Even partial restitution, when combined with sincere remorse, carries weight with courts
6

Prepare the Guilty Plea Strategy

The Chinese guilty plea and punishment acceptance system (认罪认罚从宽) offers significant sentence reductions:

  • 10-30% sentence reduction depending on acceptance timing
  • Early acceptance during investigation yields maximum reduction
  • Requires truthful confession, acceptance of charges, and agreement to proposed sentence
  • Your lawyer will advise whether this system is appropriate for your case
