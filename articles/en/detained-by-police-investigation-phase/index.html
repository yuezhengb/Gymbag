Criminal Defense Police Investigation PRC Criminal Procedure

Executive Summary

When a family member or corporate executive is suddenly detained by the Chinese Public Security Bureau (PSB) from their home or office, the immediate reaction is often panic. In the Chinese criminal justice system, the Investigation Phase (侦查阶段)—the period immediately following detention—is the absolute critical window for defense. Engaging a professional defense counsel during this golden window is vital to safeguarding the detainee's constitutional rights and mitigating long-term legal exposure.

I. The Unique and Independent Status of Defense Counsel

In many Western jurisdictions, a defense attorney acts strictly as the "agent" of the client, bound closely by the client's instructions. Under the PRC Criminal Procedure Law, however, a criminal defense lawyer occupies a highly unique, independent legal status (独立诉讼地位).

This independence means that a defense attorney's duties are to the law and the facts, free from undue interference by the investigating authorities, and importantly, not strictly bound by the subjective will of the detainee.

The Western Resonance: Think of this as an institutional layer of Due Process. For example, even if a suspect, under immense psychological pressure, decides to plead guilty and accept punishment (认罪认罚), the defense lawyer retains the independent right to argue for an acquittal in court if they believe the evidence is flawed or that no crime was committed.

II. Immediate Action Items: What a Lawyer Can Do During the Investigation Phase

Time is of the essence. Unlike standard civil litigation, early intervention in a criminal matter can alter the entire trajectory of a case. During this initial phase, a qualified defense attorney is legally empowered to:

  • Conduct Detention Center Visits (会见): Establish immediate communication with the detainee at the detention center (看守所) to understand the facts, provide vital legal counsel, and assess their physical and mental well-being.
  • Inquire on Charges: Formally engage with the PSB to ascertain the specific statutory charges, the general facts alleged, and the scope of the investigation.
  • File Procedural Challenges & Grievances: Submit formal complaints or appeals if the investigative authorities commit procedural violations or infringe upon the detainee's legal rights.
  • Apply for Bail / Alteration of Compulsory Measures (取保候审): Aggressively petition for the suspect's release on bail or a downgrade of detention measures, heavily utilizing the window before a formal arrest is approved.
  • Submit Exculpatory Evidence: Collect and present favorable evidence or formal legal opinions arguing for innocence or a lesser offense directly to the prosecutors and police before formal charges are locked in.

III. Crucial Procedural Timelines: Guarding Against Arbitrary Detention

Under Chinese law, the power to detain is strictly regulated by statutory clocks. Understanding these timelines is paramount to identifying abuses of power:

Summons and Compulsory Summons (传唤/拘传): The police cannot hold a suspect under a standard summons for more than 12 hours. In exceptionally major or complex cases, this may be extended to a maximum of 24 hours.

Notification of Custody (拘留通知): Upon formally placing a suspect under criminal detention, the PSB is statutorily required to notify the family within 24 hours, except where notification would hinder the investigation.

If these deadlines are crossed without proper legal transition, it constitutes a form of procedural non-compliance akin to a violation of Habeas Corpus. A defense lawyer will immediately intervene to challenge the lawfulness of the prolonged detention.

IV. The Corporate Risk Horizon: High-Yield Criminal Exposures

Criminal risk is not confined to individuals; corporate entities and their executives in China face strict regulatory criminal enforcement. The most prevalent white-collar crimes frequently targeted by the state include:

  • Falsely Issuing Special VAT Invoices (虚开增值税专用发票) – A severe tax fraud offense
  • Occupational Encroachment / Embezzlement (职务侵占) – Insiders misappropriating corporate assets
  • Misappropriation of Corporate Funds (挪用资金)
  • Contract Fraud (合同诈骗)
  • Illegal Absorption of Public Deposits (非法吸收公众存款)

Implementing robust Corporate Criminal Compliance (刑事合规) programs is the best ex-ante defense. However, if an investigation is already underway, a defense counsel will work to ring-fence corporate liability from personal executive liability.

Conclusion

When the state exercises its police powers, relying on informal networks, personal connections, or "favors" (Guanxi) is a dangerous gamble that frequently backfires. The most effective shield is the immediate mobilization of professional, transparent legal defense. Under the law, everyone stands equal; through rigorous, independent advocacy, defense counsel ensures that the scales of justice remain balanced.`,
